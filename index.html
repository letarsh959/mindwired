Mind Wired

Mind Wired is a one-person psychology education channel. Each post explains one peer-reviewed finding about human behaviour, as a short narrated video or a numbered photo carousel ("Mind Glitch #N").

The Mind Wired publishing tool posts the channel's own original content to the channel's own TikTok account (@mindwired90) on a daily schedule, after the account owner has authorised it through TikTok's consent screen. It does not post to anyone else's account and does not publish third-party content.
