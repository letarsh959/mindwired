Mind Wired

Mind Wired is a one-person psychology education channel. Each post explains one peer-reviewed finding about human behaviour, as a short narrated video or a numbered photo carousel ("Mind Glitch #N").

The Mind Wired publishing tool

The Mind Wired publishing tool is a private app used only by the channel's owner. After the owner signs in through the platform's own consent screen, it:

  • uploads the channel's own original videos to the Mind Wired YouTube channel, and reads that channel's own video statistics and analytics to decide which topics to make next;
  • posts and pins a question under the channel's own videos and replies to comments left on them;
  • posts the channel's own photo carousels to the Mind Wired TikTok account (@mindwired90).

It does not act on anyone else's account, is not offered to the public, and does not publish third-party content.
