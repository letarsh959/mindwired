Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy covers the Mind Wired publishing tool ("the app") and this website. The app is used only by the owner of the Mind Wired channel, for the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

YouTube and Google data

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an access token and a refresh token for the Mind Wired channel and may access:

  • the channel's own videos: uploading new videos and setting their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists;
  • the channel's own public statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, audience country) for its own videos;
  • comments on the channel's own videos, including the public display name and text of viewers who comment there, so the app can post replies and pin the channel's own comments.

This data is used only to publish the channel's content, choose future topics from its own performance, and answer comments on its own videos. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not used to build profiles of viewers. The app does not access any other channel's private data.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok data

When the owner authorises the app on TikTok, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) for the Mind Wired account, plus the posting options TikTok allows. These are used only to publish Mind Wired's own posts to that account.

Where it is stored and for how long

Tokens, statistics and comment text are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published. Statistics and comment copies are kept while the channel runs and deleted within 30 days of a request or of the channel closing. The photos served from this site are Mind Wired's own public post images.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings → Third-party access. TikTok access can be revoked in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. A viewer who wants a copy of their comment removed from the owner's computer can ask through the contact link below; deleting the comment on YouTube also removes it from the app's next read.
