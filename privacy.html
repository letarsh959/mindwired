Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

What we collect

When the Mind Wired account owner authorises the app, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) for that one account. The app also reads the creator information TikTok provides for posting (such as the available privacy settings).

How we use it

Only to publish Mind Wired's own posts to the Mind Wired TikTok account and to show the posting options TikTok allows. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are never published, sold or shared. The photos served from this site are Mind Wired's own public post images.

Other people's data

The app does not collect, store or process data about any other TikTok user or any viewer.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking deletes the stored tokens' usefulness; the owner also deletes them locally.
